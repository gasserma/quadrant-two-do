# Play Store listing: Quadrant Two Do

## App name (30 max)

Quadrant Two Do

## Short description (80 max)

Recurring tasks that show how close each one is to due. Important, not urgent.

## Full description (4000 max)

Some things are never urgent until suddenly they are: flossing, the furnace filter, the dentist,
calling your grandmother. The Eisenhower matrix calls these quadrant two: important, but not
urgent. Quadrant Two Do is a simple checklist for them.

Give each task a period, like every day, every three weeks or twice a year. The most overdue task
sits at the top. Each task's mark fills one square per quarter of its period, and the last square,
quadrant two, fills when it's due. Its colour changes as it gets later: teal to amber to coral, or
one of eight other colour schemes.

• Tap a task's mark to log it as done today
• See every time you did it, and whether you were early, on schedule or late
• Set periods in years, weeks and days
• Home-screen widget with your tasks, most urgent first, with adjustable background
• Light and dark themes, following your phone or your choice
• Nine colour schemes, including one that's easier with colour blindness, all readable in both themes
• Export a backup file, and import it on another phone
• Moves to a new phone with Android's own backup

No accounts, no ads, no tracking and no nagging notifications. The app doesn't have internet
access, so your data stays on your phone.
